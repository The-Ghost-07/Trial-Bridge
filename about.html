FAQ

Is payment real?

No. This is a front-end prototype: no money is collected and no organizer is notified until a backend and payment gateway are connected.

What does the NEW badge mean?

A package created in the last 14 days.

What does Verified Organizer mean?

It should only be given after real checks by the platform. Packages created through the Organize page are always labelled New Organizer.

Where is my data stored?

Only in this browser (localStorage). Clearing browser data removes it.
